DRRM STORYBOOK PORTAL
Schools Division Office of Romblon

HOW TO OPEN
1. Double-click index.html to open the portal.
2. Select a story card to open the animated story reader.
3. Use Upload PDF to choose any PDF from your computer and view it as a flipbook.

FILES
- index.html: main story portal
- reader.html: illustrated storybook reader
- portal.css / portal.js: portal design and PDF flipbook controls
- styles.css: story reader design
- assets: story illustrations, branding, and the bundled PDF reader

PRIVACY
Uploaded PDFs are processed inside your browser and are not sent to a website.
